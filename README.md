**Engineering at the edge of what's possible. First principles only.**

I build **systems that scale to humanity's ambitions** — infrastructure, tools, and platforms that don't just work, but redefine constraints. No clones. No tutorials. Only real-world deployments where trade-offs in performance, reliability, resilience, and human impact are laid bare.

### My Philosophy
> "The best way to predict the future is to build it."  
> — Peter Thiel (echoed by those who ship)

I don’t chase hype. I chase **fundamentals** — the immutable laws of physics, information, and human behavior.

- **First Principles Thinking** (Musk): Break every problem down to atoms and rebuild.
- **Think Different** (Jobs): Simplicity on the other side of complexity. Obsess over elegance and correctness.
- **Long-term Orientation** (Bezos/Page/Brin): Be willing to be misunderstood for years if the mission is right.
- **Open & Meritocratic** (Torvalds): Share the source. Let the code speak. Earn respect through results.
- **Ambitious Platforms** (Gates/Zuck): Build systems that empower millions — not just solve today's problem, but unlock tomorrow's possibilities.
- **High Standards** (all of them): Good is not enough. Great is table stakes. Extraordinary is the goal.

> "I don’t collect tech stacks — I collect scars, lessons, and hard-won clarity."

Great engineering is **constraint-aware**. It respects physics, economics, and human limits while refusing to be defined by them.

### What I'm Building
- **High-performance, fault-tolerant infrastructure** using modern primitives (Cloudflare, Kubernetes, advanced networking) that can survive real-world chaos.
- **Systems that expose truth**: deep observability, rigorous testing, and architectural transparency so others can learn faster.
- **Tools for ambitious builders** — not toys, but force multipliers for those solving hard problems.
- **Open source where impact > ego**: contributions that advance collective knowledge in systems, performance, and reliability.
- **Documentation of reality**: detailed post-mortems, trade-off analyses, and "why this broke in production" stories.

Inspired by SpaceX reusability, Google's moonshots, Linux's staying power, and Amazon's customer-obsessed iteration — I ship with urgency but think in decades.
